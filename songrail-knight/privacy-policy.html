Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026
App: Songrail Knight
Bundle ID: com.explorerforge.songrailknight
Developer: Explorer Forge

Explorer Forge ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Songrail Knight ("the Game") and the services integrated with it may collect, why it is used, how it is shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Information you provide

  • Display name: the nickname you choose for your profile and leaderboard.
  • Support communications: information you voluntarily include when contacting us by email.

Game and account information

  • A guest player identifier, PlayFab ID, installation or device-linked identifier used to provide cloud features.
  • Scores, leaderboard rank, stage progress, unlocked levels, Gold balance, owned and equipped heroes or gear, settings, run results, and similar gameplay records.
  • Supporter Pack entitlement, purchase status, and transaction identifiers needed to grant or restore a purchase. We do not receive your full payment card information.

Device, advertising, and technical information

  • Device type, operating system, language, IP address, app version, diagnostics, crash or performance information, and interactions with ads.
  • Advertising identifiers such as IDFA only when available and permitted by your device settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • Advertising and privacy choices recorded through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Apple privacy controls.

Some game settings and progress are also stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate the Game, save and restore progress, maintain leaderboards, and display your selected profile.
  • Provide advertisements, including interstitial and optional rewarded ads, and measure ad delivery and performance.
  • Process and restore the non-consumable Supporter Pack purchase.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to privacy or support requests.

3. Advertising and Consent Choices

Songrail Knight uses Google AdMob and Google UMP. Where required, you may choose whether to consent to personalized advertising, manage individual purposes and vendors, or decline consent. Depending on your choice and region, you may receive personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

You can reopen available privacy choices from the Game's Settings screen. On iOS, you can also control tracking permission in the device's Privacy & Security settings. Rewarded ads are optional; declining or not completing one does not remove access to the base Game.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain advertising activities may be treated as "selling", "sharing", or targeted advertising under some U.S. state laws. Eligible users can opt out through the privacy options made available in the Game.

4. Service Providers

We use service providers that process information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform: advertising, consent collection, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and related services. Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft PlayFab: guest player services, private cloud save, display names, statistics, and leaderboards. Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: purchase processing, purchase history, and restoration on iOS. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies: game engine and in-app purchasing technology. Songrail Knight does not use Unity Ads. Unity Privacy Policy

5. Legal Bases and International Processing

Depending on your location and the context, we process information to provide the Game and requested purchases, with your consent, to comply with law, or for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, service operation, and improvement. Our providers may process information in countries other than where you live, subject to safeguards required by applicable law.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Local information remains until it is reset in the Game, cleared through device controls, or the Game is uninstalled.
  • Cloud progress, profile data, and leaderboard records are retained while needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or until a valid deletion request is completed.
  • Purchase and advertising information is retained according to applicable law and the policies of the relevant provider.

To request access, correction, or deletion of data associated with the Game, email us with your display name and any available PlayFab ID. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We may request limited additional information to verify the request.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing; withdraw consent; opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing; and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Songrail Knight is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the Game, our providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the Game or store listing where appropriate.

게임은 Google AdMob과 Google UMP를 사용합니다. 관련 법령이 요구하는 지역에서는 맞춤형 광고 동의, 목적·파트너별 관리 또는 동의 거부를 선택할 수 있습니다. 선택과 지역에 따라 맞춤형, 비맞춤형 또는 제한된 광고가 제공될 수 있습니다.

필요한 경우 게임의 Settings 화면에서 개인정보 선택 사항을 다시 열 수 있으며, iOS 기기의 개인정보 보호 및 보안 설정에서도 추적 권한을 관리할 수 있습니다. 보상형 광고는 선택 사항이며, 시청을 거부하거나 완료하지 않아도 기본 게임 이용은 제한되지 않습니다.

회사는 개인정보를 금전적 대가로 판매하지 않습니다. 다만 일부 미국 주 법률은 특정 맞춤 광고 활동을 개인정보의 "판매", "공유" 또는 타겟 광고로 정의할 수 있으며, 해당 이용자는 게임에서 제공되는 개인정보 설정을 통해 거부할 수 있습니다.
